# Valorant Economy guide
> In valorant the economy is quite simple compared to other First Person Tactical Shooters. Especially helped by the fact that we can see the enemies economic status at the start of the round.

## Definitions
- **Fullbuy**: A buy when you have large shield (for 1000) and a major rifle (Phantom/Vandal). This can vary but usually it is a buy worth more than 3900, and it usually includes full set of utility

## The basic round rotation
There are 5 types of round:
1. Pistol round
2. Eco(n) round
3. Bonus round
4. Force round
5. Fullbuy round

**There is a basic structure to how the rounds flow**:
1. Pistol Round
2. If you lost pistol, you have an eco round next; if you won pistol, you force next round
3. Now we are in a 4 different outcomes situation
   1. You won pistol and you won force: You Play bonus round
   2. You won pistol and you lost force: You play eco
   3. You lost pistol and you lost eco: You fullbuy
   4. You lost pistol and you won eco: You bonus
4. Now we get to the start of standard economic rotation: On 4th round everyone should be able to buy. In this specific round which is the first fullbuy for everyone, you don't have to consider next round because everyone should have as much buy as possible with maximum strength weapons
5. Winner replenishes into fullbuy; Loser plays eco, in some instances if spike was planted and economy is especially good the loser might force or even fullbuy
6. From here on the start is: Fullbuy unless you would have less than 3900 or 4100 (depends on who you ask, I personally tell people to aim for 4100 so they can replenish utility) or majority of your team cannot buy with you. People who die a lot will have much less money than people who survive won rounds. So they might buy for their teammates or if you soloq don't count on it and just run on your own econ cycle, but try to keep track of when the team is buying, when they are forcing and when they are ecoing.

### Pistol round
This happens at the start of every half of the game (not overtime rounds though).
This is a round where you cannot physically buy any weapon above the pistol grade.
The point of this round is to either win or die.

_Why is that?_
**Because when you die you get more money for the next round than if you survive**
> This is the reason when you watch pros play and if they know they cannot win the round, like in 2v5 or 1v3 clutch, they hold position near the site and run into spike explosion. This is to deny enemy the ult point but also to get more money next round. Similar logic is applied on eco and force rounds. If you have equipment worth less than you would get from dying and start of new round, you try to die to spike explosion.

_What should you buy on a pistol round?_
**Depends on the agent you are playing.**
For example **Reynas** usually buy full set of abilities and nothing more. Because if you get a kill without taking any damage you get a weapon + 50 overheal, which is worth more than any buy you can make.
On **Sova** you want to buy drone and a shield or full set of utility on attack, but shockdarts and a ghost on defense. This stems from your role of an information gatherer. On defense your drone does not do much in pistol round, but on attack it can serve as a distraction and tell your team where util or enemies are, while shockdarts are great for stalling the opponent and dealing chip damage while holding site entry.
And for last example **Sage**: You want to buy wall + shield or wall + ghost. This is usually dependant on your playstyle, if you feel aggressive for the round then you can forego the shield and buy ghost, but if you want to be secure and make sure you win the round, you might want shield to make sure you stay alive longer. Also shield is a solid option if you know you aim is good and you can hit doubletaps with classic.

### Eco(n) round
> This round usually follows a lost pistol round or losses where team was gambling the next round.

On an eco(n) round the team buys nothing but the essentials. This might be a pistol, small shield or utility. The goal is to have at minimum 4200 for next round so you can fullbuy.
