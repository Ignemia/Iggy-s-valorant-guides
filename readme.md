# Iggy's Valorant Guides
> This repository contains my attempt to put my 10 years of competitive gaming experience into readable "textbook" format. 

## Target audience
Most of these are aimed at beginner to slightly above average players. 

## How to read
I wrote these when I have spare time so there might be factual inaccuracies after game updates and perhaps meta changes.
You should take into consideration that I am aiming to provide a structural guidance more than this to be a hollistic picture. It's more a of repository (pun intended) of knowledge.

## Why? 
Because I want to provide free resources to beginner players. Currently you would have to watch youtube videos that you will forget in 20 minutes. Reading let's your brain interpret and remember better. That's why schools still use textbooks instead of just teacher reciting knowledge. 

## Should I read this? 
Depends.
If you are an ascendant player, perhaps not, most of this will feel overly simplified and prescriptive. That is on purpose, beginner players and slightly intermediate players lack the fundamental understanding of the game, so it might devolve into chaos too quickly. These guides are to help them prevent the devolving and keep the games structured + perhaps improve their understanding of important concepts and state of the game. 
If you are completely new to all games... Probably not yet, get the feel for the game. Learn the absolute basics of movement, learn what maps look like, learn what weapons are in the arsenal. 
**If you have done what is in paragraph above, then welcome. These guides are intended for you. Now you know the absolute fundamental knowledge about how the game plays and have some feel for it. Now is the time to turn feelings into actions and randon decisions into deliberate ones.**
