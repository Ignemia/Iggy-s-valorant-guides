# Ultimate abilities
> You might notice that in my economy and round stages guides I have left out any mention of ultimate abilities. This is deliberate because ultimate abilities have their own economy and place in the game

## What are ultimate abilities
> When I first came from overwatch to Valorant I felt like every aingle ultimate ability was too overpowered. Now having hundreds of hours of competitive valorant under my belt I can calmly state, that it is by design.

Ultimate abilities are non-purchasable, non-recovering abilities aimed to be the centerpoint of a play.  
 
Most ultimate abilities are charged over multiple rounds and some are even used only once per half.

This is because the abilities are definitionally aimed to be OP. When you hear Raze's ultimate voiceline, you need to know to run, it displaces the whole team for the cost of stacking for rounds.  

## The ultimate economy
Just like with money you use to purchase weaponry, shields and utilities, the ultimates have their own economy.  

You should not be hoarding the ultimates, but sometimes the right moment for using one just does not appear so you end up having 5 ultimates for a round. This is fine, it happens but don't go nuts using all of them at once unless it's the ladt round.  

Generally I see 3 rules for when to use ultimates:
1. Displacing opponents and taking space
2. Synergy with another ability or ultimate ability
3. To win the round

### Displacing opponents and taking space
In valorant controlling the map tends to be tedious job for controller players to know where to smoke, how to push their smokes etc... 

Certain ultimate abilities (like Sova's Hunter's Furt) can get space for free by fisplacing opponents. For this to occur the team needs to follow up and go take that space. When you hear the voiceline, you know enemies will be running all over the place
