# Whiffing
> This one is a doozie. We all whiff every now and again, but there are ways to mitigate many of these whiffing scenarios. And this is what this article is about. 

## What is a whiff? 
Whiff is a missed opportunity to win a fight you should win 9/10 times. That 1/10 is just enemy getting lucky or human imperfection.
These are gunfights with an opponent who can't aim, these are shots with OP on long distance when you can land your shots etc... Fight you know you can win, but just didn't this time. 

## What are the reasons for whiffing? 
Most common type of whiff is improperly preparing the battlegrounds. When you take a fight you usually want to apply debuff to your opponent, to stack the cards in your favour. The opponent holding an angle might prefire and hit a lucky headshot or perhaps has far reaction time. But when you blind them, stun them, hinder them etc... Your chances of winning go up drastically. This is the preparation for the fight. Not doing this properly can lead yo you whiffing a fight. 

Second most common type of whiff is an aim whiff. Where your aim is crisp but in this fight you just miss every shot in the clip and die while reloading. This is much harder to explain with in-game mechanics and logical reasoning, but most common reason is panicking. Not staying calm during the fight ends up making you make rushed decisions leading to you overcorrecting your aim and not landing shots.  
Second most common reason is what I call aim deterioration. This is the idea that when you are in a competitive match, 90% of the time you are not shooting or actively aiming at anything. This leads to your aim losing it's precision and then you miss your shots. To avoid this I recommend doing what can be called "a small rewarmup" before round starts. Take out your pistol and find two spots that you want to shoot at, for 5 seconds shoot at each spot and swap them around. This will keep your aim crisp for the upcoming round and if you do it before each round, for the whole game.
