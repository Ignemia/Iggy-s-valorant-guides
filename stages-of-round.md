# Stages of round
> In valorant like in any tactical shooters and any tactical game flow of a round is/should be defined by specific stages which can be categorized into time/tempo specific segments

1. Early round (information gathering and early picks)
2. Mid-late round (execute and rotation)
3. Objective focus (plant spike, hold site)
4. [Optional | dependant on outcome of stage 3] Late round (role swap)

## Let's go through each stage individually
### Stage 1: Information gathering and early picks
This is the stage in fhe round where:
**Defenders**: Peak and generally try to get idea of where the enemy is located and likely to go. This is usually for taking space and getting map control but I will talk about this sub-objective in another article.
**Attackers**: Try to shoot a peaking defenders, which would be considered an entry frag usually prompting fast rush into site or rotation, which is the attackers' IGL prerogative to decide due to the 4v5 imbalance. 

### Stage 2: Execute and rotation
information has been gathered, last ~30 seconds of the round is remaining or entry frag has been got. Now is the time to act. 
**Attackers**: Try to use as much utility to clear site and enter. It's now or never.
**Defenders**: Try to stop defenders using utility and being in a defensive position using their positional advantage, which attackers are trying to take away using utility. This is the moment controllers and initiators shine. They have to have a clear read on opponents position to flash them, flush them out or otherwise take away their positional advatange.

This loss of advantagous positioning usually prompts all defenders who are off site to start their migration towards attacked site while maintaining **defense line**.

### Stage 3: Plant and defense
Attackers have successfully got into site (usually) thanks to numbers advantage placing the defenders in (commonly 3v4) retake disadvantage. Defenders who were early in position near site are now trying to stall or completely prevent the spike being planted by using their utility and safe peaks. Attackers are trying to get the spike down by defending the spike carrier and trying to kill the early rotators.

### Stage 4: Role swap
As the stage name suggest in this situations roles are swapped.

Defenders are out of site trying to get in and defuse the spike. But most of all utility has been used in earlier stages. So only rechargable util remains to retake.

Attackers have taken on the defenders role also with pretty much only rechargable utility.

All previous stages play out again but this time without excessive utility. Raw aim is the most valuable commodity in this stage. This is when individual talent shines most.

## The defense line
> Imagine attackers are fish in the sea. defenders are fishermen using fishing net to catch them. The net has been cast, as the ship flows forward it creates a trap for the fish. and the only way for the fish is forward. 

The defense line is exactly this. Defenders close in on the attackers from all aides and try to prevent them from rotating. there is an imaginary line between each two players. They have to hold that line together as crossfire and that way you can encapsulate and trap the attackers in their position. when execute stage comes all defenders are nearby and can quickly eliminate all attackers from the front and behind. 

This is one of the most important strategic concepts in all of tactical shooter's strategy.
