# Dueling
Taking duels and 1 on 1 fights is a large indivisible part of any tactical FPS game. It's the most common way you will end up eliminating an opponent or being eliminated. This is why it is important to learn how to determine if a fight is a fair duel, and how to win a duel by any means necessary. And no; it is not all about raw aim and mechanical prowess.

## Recognizing a duel worth taking
> Some fights are not worth fighting. We know that from life experience but it even more applies in-game.

**A fight that is worth taking on is usually a fight where the reward exceeds the risk.**  
For example a fight where you are 1 on 1 with enemy carry who is 26-5 but you still have 2 teammates alive but the carry is the alst one standing is not worth taking alone. In this scenario disengaging and letting them be or just trying to stall them while your teammates rotate over is much more worth it than risking dying and leaving your teammates in an uncertain 2v1.
But taking a 1 on 1 early in the round whn you have a flash and can get a free pick on a blind opponent is worth it. But make sure to not overextend. Staying alive and not getting traded is always preferrable to the alternative. I will talk about presence and pressure in another article specifically about creating and maintaining pressure.  

## How to take a duel if you don't have teammates to increase man-power?
I personally try to always follow 5 rules of a duel:
1. **Know your opponent.** Observe them through the match and see what they do, anything predictable about their play is a benefit for you. If you know they crouch after shooting one bullet, don't aim at head level, aim at torso. They will give you the headshot for free.
2. **Know your agent.** Knowing your agents abilities, what is a good encounter and what is not is very important. A Jett dropping from heaven will most likely be able to avoid making noise on drop, so don't try to cover up the drop noise with firing (I always tell people to not do that even when they don;t play Jett but that's for another discussion all together).
3. **Know the state of the round.** There are 3 aspects to this. Your available abilities (what you can use to win the duel), Enemies available abilities (what you need to worry about and pay attention to) and positioning (know where you opponent is at all times)
4. **Make as little noise as possible.** This touches on third point in previous point, which is "know the positioning". You want to deny as much of this information as you can. So hold down the shift and crouch so they cannot see your head if you are behind cover or in a high-ground.
5. **Dealing chip damage is better than saving bullets.** If you can hit your opponents 3 times with a Vandal for 120 in span of 15 seconds instead of one big spray, it's better than not taking the shot and leaving them on full hp. An opponent with 30hp to work with is much more likely to be be careful and very jumpy. They will not try to plant or defuse the spike if they know a single shot anywhere will end them.

If you recount all of this in the "end-of-round 1v1", you will be more prepared to win than your opponent most of the time and your chances increase immensly.

## What not to do in a duel?
**Most importantly. Don't take a fight that has no value if won but would have serious consequences if lost.**  
> For example: Don't take a fight if you hear 3 people at the start of the round and you know enemy Sage has a revive. Best case scenario you kill one, which is already a 1 in 3 that it will be the Sage if there is the Sage with the group. And you will get traded and unless you get lucky and kill the Sage, she will just revive whoever you eliminated. Meaning you died for pretty much nothing.  
  
**Don't take a long range fight with a sniper**
This one is pretty self-explanatory since sniper rifles are designed for long range fights. Unless you are absolutely certain you can outaim any sniper on the opposing team with your Vandal or Guardian. You are taking a huge risk for very little reward.  
  
**Don't push smokes without utility**
> How many time has this happened? You ran into a smoke and a Clove or an Omen were inside with a judge and killed half the team in span of 3 seconds.

Clear the smoke with Fade's prowlers or Skye's dog. Maybe even just flash inside the smoke or reveal with Sova recon dart. But don't just blindly run in and push through  

## How to fight certain weapons
- **Snipers:** The best way to defeat someone playing a sniper rifle is to get very close with an SMG or a shotgun and just eliminate them by run-and-gun strategy as they cannot shoot more than one bullet and if they are scoped their aim is slowed down meaning they cannot track you properly.
- **SMGs:** Pump them full of lead with a shotgun if you are up close (good usecase for a shorty) or just take their head off with a sniper rifle from long range.
- **Shotguns:** Avoid them and if you cannot stand as far from them as possible. Shotguns efficiency decreases with the square of distance. The further you are the less effective a shotgun will be.
- **Machine guns/Heavies:** They have exactly one purpose. Supressive fire. Their goal is to hold and angle closed down. Wait until the spray stops and peek. Raw aim is extremely important as you have to hit a headshot before they can start the spray again.
